## POST 4 — 50밀리초 (교육형)

```
Customers decide whether they trust your website in 50 milliseconds.

That's 0.05 seconds. Faster than a blink. Before they read a single word.

Studies on first impressions keep landing on the same number, and it's humbling: most of the "decision" about your business happens before anyone understands what you do.

Three things that move that first impression the most:

1. Does it look made for YOU, or pulled from a template? People can feel the difference instantly.
2. Does it load fast and work on a phone? Most of your visitors are on mobile.
3. Is the next step obvious? One clear thing to tap, not ten.

You can't talk a visitor out of a bad first impression. But you can design a good one.

How fast does your site load on your own phone right now? Go check. I'll wait.

#webdesign #ux #firstimpressions #smallbusiness #branding
```
